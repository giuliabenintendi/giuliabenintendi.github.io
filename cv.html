CV

Experience & Education

Since September 2026 I'm a research assistant at the ALPI Lab, University of Zurich, working with Prof. Giorgia Ramponi on multi-agent reinforcement learning.

From March to July 2026 I was a research intern at the Collaborative AI group, University of Stuttgart, with Prof. Andreas Bulling and Constantin Ruhdorfer, investigating how joint attention can enable zero-shot coordination in cooperative multi-agent RL.

In October 2026 I completed a double M.Sc. in Industrial Automation Engineering at the University of Brescia and in Robotic Systems at Sorbonne University.

For my thesis at the MMTLab, Brescia, I developed gaze-based methods to detect eye contact in caregiver–infant interactions, as part of the VIPPSTAR project.

I received my B.Sc. in Industrial Automation Engineering from the University of Brescia in 2024 (110/110 cum laude). In my first year I received a Fondo Giovani merit award as one of the top first-year engineering students.

During my final year I was a research intern in the Control Research Group with Prof. Antonio Visioli, developing ML models to predict a patient's level of consciousness for anesthesia control (ACTIVA project).
